How live news works

Updated every two hours

The page is refreshed on a schedule, so a story can be up to two hours behind the source. Choose a time above to see what each update added.

What is included

Outages that widely affect a major service and are confirmed by the company's own status page or a major wire service, major security events, leadership changes, large acquisitions, market-moving results, and chip or quantum milestones.

What waits

Routine vendor status incidents, short blips, routine updates and small funding rounds go to the daily Signal Brief instead.

Reviewed first

Items touching conflict or politics are held for a person to review before they appear.

Financial items are news, not investment advice. Stories stay here for 24 hours.
