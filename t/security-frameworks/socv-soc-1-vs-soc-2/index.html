SOC 1 vs SOC 2

Certifications & Attestations

SOC 1 covers controls relevant to a customer's financial reporting — think payroll processors and payment platforms whose errors would distort accounts. SOC 2 covers controls relevant to security, availability and confidentiality of a service. Many providers need SOC 2; SOC 1 matters when your service touches the customer's books.

Leader lensAsking every vendor for 'SOC' without the type wastes everyone's time. Match the report to the risk the vendor actually creates for you.
