LLM-as-Judge

Evaluation & Observability

Using a language model to grade agent outputs or trajectories against a rubric, enabling evaluation at a scale humans cannot match. It is fast and consistent enough for regression testing, but it inherits model biases and must itself be calibrated against human judgements.

Key factUsed well, LLM judges grade the bulk of cases while humans audit samples and edge cases. Used alone, a system can drift into pleasing the judge rather than doing the work.
Leader lensApprove the rubric and the human audit rate, not just the judge's score. Evaluation quality is a governance decision disguised as a tooling choice.
