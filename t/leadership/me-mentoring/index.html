Mentoring

Coaching & Development

A longer relationship in which a more experienced person shares judgement, context and hard-won mistakes with someone earlier in the journey. Unlike coaching, mentoring does advise — its value is transferred experience, not just drawn-out insight. It fails when it becomes cloning: a good mentor helps the mentee become more themselves, not a smaller copy of the mentor.

Leader lensOffer one specific piece of experience this week to someone junior: a mistake you made at their stage and what it taught you. Specific and unflattering beats wise and general.
