Follow-Through

Execution

Closing loops: doing what the meeting decided, reporting back without being chased, and surfacing problems the moment a commitment is at risk. Follow-through is the least glamorous tile on this table and the one teams judge most accurately. A leader's real reputation is not their ideas; it is their ratio of promises kept to promises made.

Leader lensKeep a visible commitments list for one week — everything you promise, to anyone — and review it Friday. Tell anyone whose item will slip before the deadline, not after.
