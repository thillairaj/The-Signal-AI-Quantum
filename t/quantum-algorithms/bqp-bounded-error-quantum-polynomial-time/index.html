BQP

BQP — Bounded-Error Quantum Polynomial Time

Limits & Complexity

The complexity class of problems a quantum computer can solve in polynomial time with error probability at most 1/3 — the quantum analogue of BPP. BQP is believed to strictly contain BPP (factoring is in BQP and believed outside BPP) and to sit inside PSPACE. Crucially, BQP is not NP: there is no evidence quantum computers solve NP-complete problems efficiently, and strong evidence they do not in the black-box setting.

Key factKnown relations: BPP subset of BQP, BQP subset of PP and PSPACE; factoring and discrete log are in BQP; NP-complete problems are not believed to be.
Leader lensThe map of what quantum computing is for. Any claim that quantum machines will 'solve optimisation' wholesale contradicts the BQP picture — NP-hard problems stay hard.
