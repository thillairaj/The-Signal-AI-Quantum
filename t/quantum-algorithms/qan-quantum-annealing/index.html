QAn

Quantum Annealing

Search & Optimisation

A heuristic optimisation method that encodes a problem as an Ising model or QUBO and slowly evolves the system toward its low-energy state, using tunnelling to pass through barriers. Commercialised by D-Wave, it runs on special-purpose analogue hardware rather than a gate-model computer. Decades of benchmarking have not produced a confirmed scaling advantage over the best classical heuristics for practical problems.

Key factFormulated as minimising an Ising energy E(s) = sum of h_i s_i plus sum of J_ij s_i s_j over binary spins.
Leader lensTreat vendor claims cautiously: for many benchmark problems, tuned classical solvers (simulated annealing, tensor networks) match or beat annealers. A legitimate research platform, not a proven accelerator.
