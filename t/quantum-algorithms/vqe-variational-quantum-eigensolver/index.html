VQE

VQE — Variational Quantum Eigensolver

Quantum Machine Learning

A hybrid algorithm for ground-state energies: a parameterised circuit (the ansatz) prepares a trial state, a quantum device measures the Hamiltonian's expectation value, and a classical optimiser adjusts the parameters to minimise it, exploiting the variational principle that any trial energy upper-bounds the true ground energy. Proposed in 2014, VQE became the flagship NISQ chemistry algorithm — and a decade of work exposed its scaling problems: measurement counts explode, optimisers stall on barren plateaus, and noise biases energies.

Key factVariational principle: <psi(theta)| H |psi(theta)> >= E_ground for all parameters theta — measured energy is always an upper bound.
Leader lensVQE taught the field enormous amounts and has not delivered classically intractable chemistry. Treat it as a research platform and benchmarking tool, not a production method.
