Channel Strategy

Go-To-Market

Choosing and sequencing the routes to your customer: search, paid, partnerships, marketplaces, community, direct sales, and retiring what does not work. Channels saturate, prices rise and audiences shift, so a channel strategy is a portfolio under active management, not a one-time choice.

Key factA channel counts as working when it delivers repeatable volume at a CAC your economics can carry. A one-off spike, a launch day, a single mention, is distribution luck, not a channel.
Leader lensMaster one channel before adding the second. Most young companies die of channel dabbling: five channels at 20% effort each beat by competitors doing one at 100%.
