ICE Framework

Growth Process & Efficiency

A scoring method for ranking growth ideas by Impact, Confidence and Ease, each rated, commonly 1 to 10. Sean Ellis developed and popularised it while building growth processes, to stop teams from working on whoever argued loudest rather than whatever mattered most.

Key factICE Score = Impact x Confidence x Ease, or their average, applied consistently within one team's backlog. The absolute scores do not matter; the ranking discipline does.
Leader lensScore as a team and argue about the scores, not the ideas. The conversation the framework forces is most of its value.
