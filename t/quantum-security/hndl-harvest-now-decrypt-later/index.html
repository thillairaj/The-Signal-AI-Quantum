HNDL — Harvest Now, Decrypt Later

The Quantum Threat

An attack in which adversaries intercept and store encrypted traffic today, planning to decrypt it once a cryptographically relevant quantum computer exists. The data is captured now, so the damage is already in motion even though decryption is years away. Any information with a long confidentiality life is exposed the moment it crosses a network.

Key factThe attack needs no quantum computer today, only storage, which is why intelligence agencies treat it as a present-day collection practice, not a future hypothetical.
Leader lensAsk which of your data would still matter in ten years if read by a competitor or a state. That list is your exposure today.
