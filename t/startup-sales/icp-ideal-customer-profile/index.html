ICP

Sales ICP — Ideal Customer Profile

Sales Foundations

The firmographic and behavioural description of the accounts most likely to buy, succeed and renew: industry, company size, geography, technology in place, and the trigger events that make them ready. For sales, the ICP is a targeting tool, not a marketing slogan.

Key factA practical test: if your last ten wins share a pattern your ICP does not describe, your ICP is wrong, not your customers.
Leader lensWrite your ICP from your actual wins and losses, then tighten it. A narrow ICP you can name company-by-company beats a broad one you can only describe.
