Azure Reservations

Management, Cost & Strategy

A commitment discount: promise to use a VM, database or other resource for a one- or three-year term and Microsoft charges materially less than pay-as-you-go. Reservations suit the stable core of the estate that runs all day, every day.

Key factReservations can be exchanged or cancelled under limits, which makes the commitment less rigid than it first appears — but scope mistakes still cost real money.
Leader lensReserve the boring, always-on baseline and nothing more. Reserving for hoped-for growth converts a discount into a sunk cost.
