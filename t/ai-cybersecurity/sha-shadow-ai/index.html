Shadow AI

AI as Attack Surface

AI tools and services adopted by employees without IT or security approval: free chatbot accounts, browser extensions, transcription bots and code assistants fed with company data. Each one is an unvetted data processor and a potential leak channel. Shadow AI is usually a productivity signal, not malice — people route around slow approval processes.

Key factShadow AI is the AI-era repeat of shadow IT, with one difference: the data does not just get stored by the tool, it can be absorbed into prompts, logs and sometimes training pipelines.
Leader lensBanning chatbots wholesale rarely works; publish an approved AI tool list with a fast approval path, and shadow usage shrinks on its own.
