AI Attack Surface

AI as Attack Surface

The total set of ways an organisation's AI systems can be attacked: the models, the data they train and retrieve from, the prompts and APIs that reach them, the agents' permissions, and the vendors behind them. It grows with every AI feature shipped, often faster than security teams can map it. Unlike a traditional attack surface, parts of it accept natural language as input, which makes the boundary unusually fuzzy.

Key factEvery input an AI system accepts — text, documents, images, tool outputs — is potentially an instruction channel, which is why the AI attack surface cannot be secured with network controls alone.
Leader lensAsk for an inventory of your AI systems and their entry points before approving the next one; you cannot defend a surface you have not mapped.
