Jailbreaking

LLM & GenAI Risks

Techniques that persuade or trick a safety-trained model into producing content it was trained to refuse: role-play framings, encoded requests, multi-turn manipulation, or exploiting gaps between languages and formats. Jailbreaks are published and shared quickly, so a model's resistance decays over time even if the model itself never changes. It matters most where model output feeds people or systems directly.

Leader lensTreat safety fine-tuning as a speed bump, not a wall: the controls that matter are what the model can access and what its outputs are allowed to trigger.
