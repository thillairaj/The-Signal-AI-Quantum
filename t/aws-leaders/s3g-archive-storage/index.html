S3 Glacier — Archive Storage

Storage

The very low-cost archive tiers of S3, designed for data that must be kept for years — backups, compliance records, media archives — but is rarely read. Retrieval is slower and priced separately, with the deepest tier intended for data accessed perhaps once a year.

Key factGlacier storage classes are among the cheapest ways to store data in AWS, with the trade-off that retrieval can take minutes to hours depending on the option chosen.
Leader lensArchive tiers are how you satisfy seven-year retention duties without paying hot-storage prices. The discipline is tagging what can be archived, automatically, from day one.
