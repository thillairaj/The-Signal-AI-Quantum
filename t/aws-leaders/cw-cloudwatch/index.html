CloudWatch

Management, Cost & Strategy

The monitoring and observability service of AWS: it collects metrics, logs and events from virtually every service, raises alarms when thresholds break, and powers operational dashboards. When something fails at 3 a.m., CloudWatch is where the evidence lives.

Key factCloudWatch Logs can retain log data indefinitely, and its alarms can trigger Auto Scaling, notifications or automated remediation actions.
Leader lensMonitoring breadth is rarely the problem; alarm discipline is. If your teams get hundreds of alerts a week, CloudWatch is working and your operating model is not — fund alarm quality, not more dashboards.
