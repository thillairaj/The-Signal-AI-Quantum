ChatGPT agent (formerly OpenAI Operator)

Computer & Browser Agents

OpenAI's computer-using agent that browses and acts on websites, handling tasks such as bookings, ordering and form-filling in its own browser. It launched in January 2025 as Operator, a research preview for ChatGPT subscribers, and in July 2025 it became ChatGPT agent, fully integrated into ChatGPT; the standalone Operator site then closed.

Key factMaker: OpenAI. Launched January 2025 as Operator, a research preview powered by its Computer-Using Agent model; renamed ChatGPT agent in July 2025.
Leader lensRead it as a roadmap signal for where consumer agents are heading. For enterprise use, expect to build on computer-use capabilities with your own controls and approvals.
