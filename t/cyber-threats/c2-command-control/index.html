C2 — Command & Control

Escalation & Lateral Movement

The communication channel between malware on a victim machine and the attacker's infrastructure, used to send instructions and receive stolen data. Modern C2 hides inside ordinary-looking web traffic and trusted cloud services, which makes spotting it a matter of patterns rather than obvious bad addresses.

Key factMany intrusions are first spotted by their C2 patterns, such as machines contacting rare domains at perfectly regular intervals, known as beaconing.
Leader lensEgress control matters as much as entry control: know which systems may talk to the internet, and investigate rhythmic or unusual outbound traffic.
