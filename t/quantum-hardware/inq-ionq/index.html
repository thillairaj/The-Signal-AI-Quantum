IonQ

Platforms & Vendors

IonQ builds trapped-ion systems and was the first pure-play quantum company to list publicly, via a SPAC merger in 2021. Its Forte systems target the mid-30s in algorithmic qubits, and it sells access through all three major clouds plus directly. IonQ has also been acquiring networking and sensing companies to widen beyond computing.

Key factIonQ's metric of choice, Algorithmic Qubits, counts usable qubits for benchmark circuits rather than raw physical qubits — an honesty other vendors are slowly adopting.
Leader lensA listed company: its filings are public due-diligence material. Read the revenue mix — research contracts versus commercial demand tells the maturity story.
