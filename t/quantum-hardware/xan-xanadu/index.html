Xanadu

Platforms & Vendors

Xanadu is a Canadian photonic company whose PennyLane software is a leading tool for quantum machine learning research. Its Borealis processor demonstrated quantum computational advantage in 2022 using continuous-variable photonics. Xanadu's stated goal is a fault-tolerant photonic machine built from networked chips manufactured in semiconductor fabs.

Key factBorealis produced its samples in 36 microseconds for a task estimated at more than 9,000 years on the best classical algorithms of the time (Nature, 2022).
Leader lensXanadu pairs a real software community, PennyLane, with a hardware bet. The software traction is verifiable today; the hardware is the option value.
