Her

IBM Heron Processor

Superconducting

Heron is IBM's superconducting processor family, introduced in 2023 with 133 qubits and tunable couplers that reduce crosstalk and errors compared with its Eagle predecessor. A revised version followed with 156 qubits and further fidelity improvements. IBM positions Heron as the workhorse of its quantum data centres and its error-correction roadmap.

Key factIBM reported that Heron's tunable-coupler design significantly cut two-qubit gate errors versus the previous generation, and Heron systems are installed in the US, Europe and Japan.
Leader lensHeron shows the industry's direction: fewer headlines about counts, more about couplers, errors and systems engineering. Read vendor news in that order.
