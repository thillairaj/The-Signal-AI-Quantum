Serverless

Service Models

A model where you deploy code (functions) or services without provisioning or managing servers at all; the provider runs, scales and bills them per use. The servers still exist — they are simply no longer your problem, including when demand drops to zero and cost drops with it.

Key factServerless pricing is typically per request and per millisecond of execution: idle costs approach zero, which inverts the usual economics of always-on servers.
Leader lensServerless suits event-driven and spiky workloads beautifully and steady high-volume workloads less so. Watch unit economics at scale, and the debugging and vendor lock-in that come with it.
