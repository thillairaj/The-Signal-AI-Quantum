Cloud Computing

Cloud Foundations

Delivering computing — servers, storage, databases, networking and software — over the internet from a provider's data centres, instead of owning and running your own hardware. You rent capacity on demand, scale it up or down as needs change, and pay for what you use rather than buying for peak load in advance.

Key factThe economic shift is CapEx to OpEx: large upfront hardware purchases become a variable operating cost that follows actual usage, for better and for worse.
Leader lensCloud does not remove the need for engineering discipline; it moves it. The skills change from maintaining hardware to managing consumption, architecture and vendors.
