MTTD — Mean Time to Detect

Security Operations

Mean Time to Detect: the average time between an attacker's first action and your organisation noticing. Long detection times give attackers weeks to explore, steal and stage ransomware.

Key factMTTD and MTTR are the two numbers that summarise SOC performance. Both are meaningless without honest measurement that starts the clock at first compromise, not at first alert.
Leader lensAsk for MTTD by incident severity and watch the trend, not a single average. A falling trend is evidence your security investment is working.
