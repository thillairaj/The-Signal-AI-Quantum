Network Segmentation

Network Security

Dividing a network into separated zones with controlled traffic between them, so a compromise in one area cannot spread freely. Guest Wi-Fi, office workstations, production systems and backups should not live on one flat network.

Key factSegmentation is what turns many ransomware incidents from company-ending events into contained departmental problems: the malware simply cannot reach everything.
Leader lensAsk for the network diagram and one answer: if a single office PC is compromised tonight, what can it reach by morning? That answer is your segmentation grade.
