GEr

Gate Error Rate

Noise, Errors & Scale

The probability that an operation introduces an error, the inverse of fidelity. Lower is better; the best labs now report around 0.1 percent and below for key operations.

Key factError and fidelity are two views of one number: 99.9 percent fidelity = 10^-3 error per gate. Fault-tolerance roadmaps target physical errors around 10^-3 to 10^-4 and below on key operations, with correction handling the rest.
