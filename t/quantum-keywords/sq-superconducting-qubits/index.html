SQ

Superconducting Qubits

Hardware & Platforms

Tiny electrical circuits, cooled to near absolute zero, behaving as artificial atoms. The approach used by IBM and Google; fast gates, demanding refrigeration.

Key factThese chips operate around 0.01 kelvin, roughly 250 times colder than deep space (about 2.7 kelvin).
Leader lensThe current market leaders run this technology, which is why most cloud quantum access today means superconducting machines.
