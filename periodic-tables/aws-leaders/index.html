Six things a leader should hold on to about AWS

Most estates run on a small core

EC2, S3, VPC, IAM, RDS, Lambda and CloudWatch cover the majority of real workloads. Master the core before chasing the catalogue's long tail.

Discounts are earned by commitment

Savings Plans and reservations convert predictable usage into lower prices — up to 72% versus on-demand on AWS's published figures. Uncommitted, variable estates pay the premium rate forever.

Every managed service is a lock-in decision

Aurora, DynamoDB and Bedrock save real effort and deepen dependence in the same motion. Take those decisions explicitly, workload by workload.

Identity is the perimeter

IAM, Organizations and CloudTrail decide whether your estate is governable. Most AWS security failures are misconfigurations on the customer's side of the shared responsibility line.

Tagging is a financial control

Without enforced cost-allocation tags you cannot say who spent what, and every cost conversation becomes an argument. Tags are policy, not housekeeping.

The bill is a management report

Cost Explorer trends, budget alerts and Trusted Advisor findings together tell you how disciplined your engineering organisation is. Read them monthly.

A leader's AWS checklist

Monthly

  • Review Cost Explorer by account and service; explain the top three movements
  • Check Savings Plans coverage and utilisation against the stable baseline
  • Confirm every account has a budget alert with a named owner
  • Scan Trusted Advisor for new security and idle-resource findings

Quarterly

  • Audit IAM: no root usage, least privilege, stale access removed
  • Verify CloudTrail is on everywhere and GuardDuty has no suppressed accounts
  • Test a backup restore, not just a backup report
  • Re-run a Well-Architected review on one top workload and close high-risk items
  • Review the service inventory for sprawl: new services adopted, duplicates retired

An honest note

This table is a map, not a recommendation. AWS offers several ways to solve most problems, and the right choice depends on your team's skills, your existing commitments and your tolerance for lock-in. Positions are as of October 2026 and services evolve quickly — verify before you commit budget.
