AI & Cybersecurity

Where artificial intelligence changes both attack and defence — the new attack surface, AI-powered attacks, and the controls and frameworks leaders need.

Core principle: AI lowers the attacker's cost and raises the defender's speed at the same time, so the contest rewards whoever adopts it with discipline. The organisations most at risk are not those attacked by AI, but those adopting AI without securing it. Treat every AI system you deploy as both a tool and a target.

Six things a leader should hold on to

Every AI system is two things at once

Each deployment is a capability for you and a target for someone else. Budget, review and monitor it as both, from the first pilot onwards.

Identity is the battleground

Stolen credentials, over-privileged agents and synthetic identities decide modern incidents. Least privilege for humans and AI agents alike is the highest-leverage habit in this table.

Process beats recognition

Deepfakes defeat faces and voices; they do not defeat callbacks, code words and dual approval. Any workflow that authorises value on recognition alone needs redesign.

Prompt injection has no patch

It is managed by architecture — least privilege, validated outputs, sandboxing, human approval on consequential actions — not by hoping the model refuses next time.

AI defence is a speed multiplier, not a substitute

AI triage, detection and prioritisation make good security teams faster. They do not replace the fundamentals: patching, identity hygiene, backups, rehearsed response.

Frameworks already exist — use them

OWASP LLM Top 10 for applications, MITRE ATLAS for threat modelling, NIST AI RMF for governance. Adopting a recognised scaffold beats inventing your own vocabulary.

A leader's AI-security checklist

Know your estate

  • Maintain an AI asset inventory with a named owner for every system, model and agent
  • Publish an approved AI tools list to drain shadow AI
  • Require provenance and data-use answers from every AI vendor
  • Classify vector stores and training data at the level of the source documents

Control the consequences

  • Scope agent permissions to the task, time-limit them, and test the kill switch
  • Validate all model output before it reaches code, queries, browsers or tools
  • Red-team AI applications before launch and after major changes
  • Add AI scenarios to incident response playbooks and exercises

The honest note

AI has not repealed the old rules: most organisations will still be breached through phishing, unpatched systems and weak identity long before an exotic model attack matters. Secure the AI you adopt, adopt AI that helps you secure — and keep doing the unglamorous fundamentals that decide most outcomes.
