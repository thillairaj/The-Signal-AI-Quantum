      Where the time goes

      A first visit, broken into its costs. Round trips are bounded by distance, which is the whole reason caches and edge servers exist. The ranges are typical, from the sources below; every path differs.

      DNS lookupoften near 0, cached
      Opening the connection1 round trip
      TLS 1.3 handshake1 round trip
      Server work50 to 300 ms, typical
      Download and renderingthe rest

      HTTP/3 merges the connection and locking steps into one setup, which is why the newest sites feel a beat quicker on mobile networks.

      Content delivery networks

      Cloudflare · Akamai · Fastly

      Job
      Cache content close to every visitor
      Traffic
      Most web traffic now passes through CDNs
      Bonus
      Attack filtering and load balancing at the edge
      • Major sites such as Facebook, Netflix and Amazon are served through CDNs.
      • If one edge location fails, routing sends traffic to the next healthy one.

      HTTP/3 and QUIC

      The newest road

      Runs on
      QUIC over UDP, not TCP
      Setup
      Transport and encryption negotiated together
      Streams
      Independent: one stalled file blocks nothing else
      • HTTP/3 keeps the same requests and responses, on a faster road underneath (RFC 9114).
      • TLS 1.3 is built into QUIC, so the locking step and the connection step merge.
