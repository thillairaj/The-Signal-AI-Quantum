NVIDIA changes chief executive, Microsoft 365 suffers a global outage, and IBM details a 1,040 qubit plan

Friday 14 November 2026 · The week of Saturday 8 to Friday 14 November 2026 · About 3 minutes to read

This was a week when the business of AI moved as fast as the technology. NVIDIA announced a new chief executive, a network fault at Microsoft took Microsoft 365 down across 74 countries, and IBM set out its next quantum processors, while Anthropic released new flagship models. Here is what happened, and what is new on The Signal.

This week in Quantum & AI

NVIDIA announces a chief executive transition

NVIDIA said on 12 November that Jensen Huang has resigned as chief executive, with Chief Financial Officer Colette Kress serving as interim CEO and Harvey C. Jones becoming the first Chair of the Board. The company says its operations and product roadmap continue without interruption, and its next results are due on 17 November. Not investment advice.

Source: NVIDIA

Microsoft 365 outage disrupts 74 countries

A Microsoft 365 outage that began on 12 November disrupted Outlook, Teams and Copilot for users in 74 countries, with more than 130,000 reports logged on the outage tracker Downdetector at the peak. Microsoft traced the fault to a network capacity upgrade and a recent network change, said there was no evidence of malicious activity, and confirmed services were fully restored on 14 November.

Source: Microsoft

Learn how cloud platforms are run and recovered in the Cloud and DevOps learning space

Anthropic releases Claude Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2

Anthropic released two flagship models: Fable 5.2, available now, and Mythos 5.2, limited to vetted organizations. In testing before the release, Fable designed three working electronic devices from scratch: a home voice assistant hub, a USB speakerphone and a handheld virtual pet, each with its own circuit board ready to build in under a day. A team of 1,000 Fable agents also turned the proof of Fermat's Last Theorem into a form a computer can check step by step, finishing in 30 hours. An earlier version of Claude took 11 days to do the same.

Source: Anthropic

IBM details Nighthawk r3 and a 1,040 qubit plan

IBM's new Nighthawk r3 processor is designed to run 10,000 quantum operations across 520 programmable qubits, using 2,034 physical qubits in total. In 2027, IBM plans to link two r3 processors into a single system of 1,040 qubits.

Source: IBM
