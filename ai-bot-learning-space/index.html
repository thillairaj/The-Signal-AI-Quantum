    Meet the Agents

    Five always-on agents, five different bets — hover or tap a name for details.

    Grok Bot

    xAI / SpaceXAI

    A team of always-on AI agents — persistent coworkers on cloud computers that sign into your tools and finish multi-step jobs.

    Launched
    Beta Aug 11, 2026
    Access
    Bundled with SuperGrok and Cursor plans
    Best for
    Developers, business teams, enterprises

    A roster of role-specific bots — a sales bot updates the CRM from call transcripts, an ops bot onboards hires and processes invoices out of Gmail, an engineering bot reproduces bugs, files tickets and hands fixes to a debugging bot.

    Bots coordinate in group chats, run scheduled routines, and learn a workflow by watching you do it once. For personal use, its Finance integration (read-only via Plaid) finds recurring subscriptions and price hikes. Limits: still in beta; cloud computers US-only during beta; all bots in an account share one computer.

    Official product page →

    Meta Muse

    Meta

    A personal AI agent that does tasks for you — books, emails, shops — and keeps working after you close the app.

    Launched
    Sept 8, 2026
    Access
    Free tier (100M tokens/week); $20/$100 plans; US and Canada
    Best for
    Individuals, households, small business

    Day-to-day personal work — books travel, sends email, fills forms, shops and checks out (always asks before paying), manages calendar and reminders, remembers your details and makes unprompted suggestions, works from its own app and WhatsApp.

    Official/project work — plans around longer-term goals and coordinates across connected apps (Slack, Notion, Shopify); the Small Business version connects ad accounts, QuickBooks and Asana to review sales performance, draft campaigns and flag expenses. Nothing is sent or spent without your approval. Runs on a dedicated Muse Secure VM; a separate Sentinel agent governs its internet access.

    Official product page →

    OpenAI dots

    OpenAI

    Always-on agents in ChatGPT — give one a goal and it keeps working in the background across days.

    Launched
    Sept 29, 2026 at DevDay
    Access
    ChatGPT Pro and Business Premium (first dot included); Enterprise beta
    Best for
    Pro users and businesses

    Each dot gets its own cloud computer and browser, 4,000+ app plugins, and works through ChatGPT, Slack and Teams. Personal use: delegate-and-forget tasks plus proactive read-only research across your apps — it catches a forgotten invoice or a bug mentioned in Slack and reports back.

    Project work: monitors customer feedback, prepares fixes, tests them and opens pull requests; updates launch materials when scope changes; reruns research as new data arrives; turns interview transcripts into clips and drafts. Specialist dots (enterprise pilots) own defined jobs like procurement, invoicing and customer support. Powered by GPT-6 Astra.

    Official product page →

    Claude

    Anthropic

    Anthropic's agentic AI — Claude Code for developers, Claude Cowork for knowledge work.

    Launched
    Claude Code available now (terminal, IDE, desktop, web); Cowork in research preview on macOS
    Access
    Subscription and API
    Best for
    Developers and knowledge workers

    Claude Code reads codebases, edits across files, runs commands, does automated code review and pull requests, refactors, and runs parallel subagents. Claude Cowork does outcome-based knowledge work in your local files — organising files, building documents and spreadsheets, analysing data; you describe the outcome and it works on its own.

    Personal: file organisation, documents, data analysis. Project work: engineering teams and knowledge-work automation.

    Official product page →

    Copilot Autopilot

    Microsoft

    A persistent AI coworker inside Microsoft 365 with its own corporate identity.

    Launched
    Late Sept 2026
    Access
    Microsoft 365 enterprise licensing
    Best for
    Enterprises and compliance-heavy organisations

    Retains memory, monitors Teams and Outlook communication, and carries multi-day assignments without human intervention. Each instance gets its own Entra identity with email, calendar, OneDrive and Teams access.

    Demonstrated running three agents at once: contract review in Word, forecasting in Excel, scheduling in Outlook. Governed through Agent 365 with audit logs and policy controls. Barely a personal product — enterprise-first by design.
