Google DeepMind launches AlphaGenome 2 to predict DNA effects in single cell types

Google DeepMind has announced AlphaGenome 2, a DNA sequence model that predicts how genetic variants affect specific human cell types rather than broad tissue averages. Trained on more than 100 million individual cell profiles across 68 tissues, it identified nearly twice as many causal variants on a single-cell immune dataset, 30% against 16% for the original model. The model is available now for non-commercial research through the AlphaGenome API, and in private preview for commercial research.

WHY IT MATTERS FOR TECH

Cell-level variant prediction helps researchers connect disease-linked DNA changes to the exact cells where they act, which broad tissue models miss.
