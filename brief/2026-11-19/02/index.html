OpenAI agents find working exploits for 948 Linux kernel flaws for about $1.2 million

OpenAI says an automated pipeline reproduced 1,724 Linux kernel vulnerabilities and generated working exploits for 948 of them, at about $1.2 million in API costs, in a November 4 technical report titled A thousand fewer paths to root. Human security researchers reviewed the findings before they went to Linux kernel maintainers, and the company notes the cost is an upper bound because the harness was not tuned for cost. Sam Altman, speaking at the New York Times DealBook Summit, said OpenAI is the only company that has the entire intelligence stack.

WHY IT MATTERS FOR TECH

Agents that reproduce and exploit real kernel flaws for about $1.2 million change the economics of vulnerability research for defenders and attackers alike.
