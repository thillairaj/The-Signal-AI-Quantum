Signal blocks screenshots so AI agents cannot read private chats

Signal is now restricting screenshots in its Android and iOS apps by default to stop operating-system AI agents from reading private chats off the screen. On iOS 27 the app blocks screenshots and adds a Block Siri AI setting, while on Android it turns on screen security when it detects an AI assistant trying to scan the display. Signal concedes the protection is incomplete, since agents can still reach data already stored on the device through the file system, and it is asking Apple, Google and Microsoft to give developers a way to keep agents out of their apps.

WHY IT MATTERS FOR TECH

Encryption protects messages in transit, not text already on screen, so messaging apps are having to defend the display itself as operating systems build agents into every surface.
