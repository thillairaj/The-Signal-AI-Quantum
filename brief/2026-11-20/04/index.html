BMO reports quantum utility in machine learning with IBM computers

BMO says it has demonstrated quantum utility in a machine learning application run on IBM quantum computers, including the IBM Quantum Nighthawk r2 processor. Built with Democritus University of Thrace, the method picks the most useful variables in a dataset and matched the quality of conventional feature selection while using roughly one tenth to one hundredth as many data samples, according to BMO. The bank cites client modelling, risk assessment and more personalised banking as possible applications.

WHY IT MATTERS FOR TECH

A bank showing a quantum method matching classical quality with far less data is a concrete step toward quantum tools earning a place in financial modelling.
