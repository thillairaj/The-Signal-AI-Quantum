UK cyber agency reports a marked rise in AI-assisted attacks since September

The UK National Cyber Security Centre said on 24 November it has seen a marked rise in AI-assisted cyber attacks since September, with incidents across several sectors involving agentic penetration-testing frameworks repurposed by attackers, most often ARTEX. The weaknesses being exploited are not new — leaked repository credentials, misconfigured infrastructure-as-code, default credentials, API flaws and over-privileged accounts — but AI tools now find and exploit them faster and more cheaply. The NCSC said most of the activity could be stopped by basic controls and best practice.

WHY IT MATTERS FOR TECH

It separates the new speed of attacks from the old weaknesses behind them, putting the fix on hygiene and technical debt rather than on new defences alone.
