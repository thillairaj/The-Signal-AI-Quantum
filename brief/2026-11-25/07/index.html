CIA and ODNI networks hit in a breach of unclassified intelligence material

The CIA and the Office of the Director of National Intelligence recently discovered a breach that exposed some unclassified information, the New York Times reported on 24 November, citing people familiar with the matter. The intrusion affected a network used to draw intelligence insights from public, unclassified material. It was not clear what information was compromised, and an ODNI official said there was no disruption to critical capabilities or classified systems.

WHY IT MATTERS FOR TECH

A breach of the system that mines open-source material shows that unclassified networks still carry intelligence value, even when classified systems are untouched.
