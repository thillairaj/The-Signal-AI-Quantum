IBM and AMD open source a GPU decoder for quantum error correction

IBM Research and AMD have open sourced Pelagos, a GPU accelerated framework for testing quantum low density parity check codes at supercomputing scale, released under an Apache 2.0 licence. The framework runs on AMD, NVIDIA and Intel GPUs and reached up to 97 times the speed of a single CPU core in single GPU tests, with 3,845 decodes per second on eight AMD Instinct MI355X GPUs. The release is part of a joint error correction codesign platform IBM, AMD and Oak Ridge National Laboratory set up earlier in November.

WHY IT MATTERS FOR TECH

Testing error correction codes needs billions of simulated error patterns, so a fast open decoder that runs on any major GPU lets more teams measure codes that could make quantum computers reliable.
