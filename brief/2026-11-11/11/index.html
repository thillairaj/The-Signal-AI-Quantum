D-Wave revenue rises 21% as bookings and contracted revenue climb

D-Wave reported third-quarter revenue of $4.5 million, up 21% from a year earlier, with nine-month bookings of $37.4 million, up 611%, and contracted revenue still to be recognised of $38.3 million. The company signed a definitive agreement with the US Department of Commerce giving it access to up to $100 million in CHIPS and Science Act funding, in instalments tied to milestones, to develop larger annealing and gate-model machines. NTT DOCOMO deployed a second production application using D-Wave's hybrid quantum technology for mobile network operations.

WHY IT MATTERS FOR TECH

Rising bookings and a large contracted-revenue balance, alongside federal funding for bigger machines, show a quantum vendor converting pilot deployments into paid production work.
