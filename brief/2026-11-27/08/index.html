Global investors line up to scout Australia's quantum industry at its 2027 conference

Quantum Australia said international investment firms including the Novo Nordisk Foundation, ACTAI Ventures, Serendipity Capital and Playground Global will attend its February 2027 conference in Sydney to explore co-investment in Australian quantum companies. The 2027 event is expected to draw more than 1,000 delegates, up from over 800 attendees and 18 international delegations in 2026, with an added Asia-Pacific startup competition. The move follows a year in which Australian quantum companies drew global attention, including Diraq advancing to the final stage of DARPA's utility-scale assessment program and 26 new quantum companies emerging from national commercialisation programs in 18 months.

WHY IT MATTERS FOR TECH

It shows capital following quantum capability into new regions, and gives Australian startups a direct route to global investors and customers.
