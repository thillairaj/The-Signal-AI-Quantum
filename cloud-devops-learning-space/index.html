What about new videos?

This room is a curated snapshot — a hand-picked selection of cloud and DevOps videos from the official cloud channels and trusted trainers, last reviewed 26 October 2026. This room is checked twice a week and new videos from the official channels are added as they appear.

One gap worth knowing: vendor keynotes often stream on event channels rather than the main ones, so this room favours teachable sessions over launch keynotes. To never miss a release, subscribe to the official Amazon Web Services, Microsoft Azure, Google Cloud Tech and CNCF channels, plus TechWorld with Nana, KodeKloud and freeCodeCamp, and turn on notifications.
